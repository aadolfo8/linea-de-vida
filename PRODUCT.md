# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is close family members reviewing memories together.

## Product Purpose

Línea de vida is a private photo archive. Its primary outcome is to balance a contemplative, evocative experience with fast discovery of moments.

## Positioning

It integrates Google Drive in read-only mode and works only with folders that each user explicitly chooses.

## Operating Context

It is a web monorepo using Next.js, NestJS, PostgreSQL, and Redis. Each user authorizes and accesses their own photo archive.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Integrates Google Drive exclusively in read-only mode.
- Uses only explicitly selected Google Drive folders; it does not perform global exploration.
- Preserves the actual precision of dates: `YEAR`, `MONTH`, `DAY`, and `UNKNOWN`.
- Preserves privacy and authorization per user.
- Does not include AI/ML, OCR, facial recognition, Google Drive writes, or social features.

## Brand Commitments

The binding visual references provided are Locomotive, Dogstudio, Immersive Garden, and Unseen/CLOU. They serve as inspiration for modern interactivity and scrolling, without copying code, layouts, images, or assets.

## Available Evidence

No data, users, license, people, concrete visual decisions, or additional assets have been confirmed to support new claims.

## Product Principles

- Protect the privacy of the archive and each user's authorization.
- Preserve the temporal fidelity of every memory.
- Support both contemplation of memories and quick discovery.
- Limit Drive integration to explicitly selected folders and read-only operations.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Accessibility must be preserved.
